Strictly segregated accounting for Dairy and Rice operations with COGS vs. OPEX tracking.

DAILY Statement for Today
Accounting Rules: (1) Revenue from delivered orders. (2) COGS from direct purchases (raw milk / paddy) avoiding double-counting. (3) OPEX from operational expenses. (4) Gross Profit = Revenue - COGS. (5) Net Operating Profit = Gross Profit - OPEX. (6) When Revenue is ₹0, calculations are flagged incomplete to prevent misleading profit metrics.
